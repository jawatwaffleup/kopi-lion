# KOPI LION — Brand foundation v0.1

## Brand idea
Nanyang kopi, brewed with precision.

## Position
A focused, modern kopi counter: fast, warm, proud and consistent. Not a Western speciality café, retro theme park or luxury house.

## Promise
The same bold cup, every time.

## Primary line
Tradition, brewed precisely.

## Voice
Direct, warm, precise and quietly proud. Use short sentences and concrete descriptions. Avoid luxury language, forced slang and unsupported claims.

## Identity
The initial lion is an original geometric concept built for the baseline. It uses a radial mane, composed face and one-colour construction. It must be tested at 12 mm and refined before trademark filing or physical production.

## Core colours
- Lion Red: #EF3340
- Action Red: #C51F30 (accessible digital controls)
- White: #FFFFFF
- Kopi Paper: #F5F1EA
- Roast Brown: #3B2318
- Condensed Cream: #E8D5B7
- Ink: #1A1A1A

Print values remain provisional until proofed on actual substrates.

## Type
- Display: Archivo
- Body: Inter

Google Fonts are loaded for the baseline. Self-host approved font files before a performance-sensitive production launch.

## Product language
- Kopi O — Bold black kopi with sugar.
- Kopi C — Kopi with evaporated milk and sugar.
- Kopi O Kosong — Bold black kopi, served without sugar.
- Kaya Toast — Crisp toast layered with kaya and butter.

Descriptions remain provisional until recipes and allergen statements are approved.

## Photography
Natural, documentary and warm without yellow filters. Show real process, steam, hands, counters and place. Capture vertical and horizontal versions. Do not use generic café stock photography.

## Production gates
Do not print packaging until taste, ingredients, product names, legal copy, dielines, logo and physical red proofs are approved.
