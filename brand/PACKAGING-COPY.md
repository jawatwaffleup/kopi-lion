## Short statement
Roasted for depth. Built for a bold, aromatic cup.

## Required back-panel fields
- Final product name
- Ingredients, in legal order
- Allergens
- Net weight
- Brewing instructions
- Storage instructions
- Batch or lot code
- Roast, packed-on or best-before field
- Manufacturer
- Packer
- Importer or distributor, if relevant
- Country of origin
- Customer contact
- Barcode
- Required certification marks

## Blocked claims
Do not publish “halal,” origin claims, nutritional claims, roast ingredients or shelf life until documented and approved.

## Supplier request
Obtain AI/PDF dielines, material specification, zipper and valve positions, bleed, safe area, print method, ink limits, colour profile, finishes and minimum order quantity.
